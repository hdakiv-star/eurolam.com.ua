Production Process

Engineered parquet boards are manufactured using advanced woodworking technologies and multi-stage quality control procedures. The natural oak top layer provides an authentic appearance and excellent wear resistance.

The multi-layer construction enhances dimensional stability and minimizes the risk of deformation caused by changes in temperature and humidity. This makes engineered parquet boards a reliable solution for both residential and commercial applications.

Finished products undergo quality inspections throughout the production process and comply with high standards for modern flooring solutions.

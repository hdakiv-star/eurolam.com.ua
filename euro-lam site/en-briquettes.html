Production Process

NESTRO briquettes are produced by compressing dried wood raw materials under high pressure without the use of chemical additives or binding agents.

During the pressing process, natural lignin contained in wood provides the strength and integrity of the finished briquettes. The finished products undergo quality control and are packaged for storage and transportation.

About the Company

EURO-LAM LLC is a Ukrainian manufacturing company located in Kozova, Ternopil Region. The company specializes in wood processing and the production of high-quality wood products.

Our Products

The company manufactures wood products for flooring, interior finishing, furniture production and use as eco-friendly solid fuel.

Lamella

Oak blanks used for the production of parquet and engineered flooring.

Engineered Parquet Board

A multi-layer flooring material with a natural oak top layer.

Modular Oak Parquet

Decorative oak parquet with various modular layout patterns.

Oak Lam Parquet

Natural oak parquet flooring combining durability, elegance and timeless design.

NESTRO Fuel Briquettes

Eco-friendly solid fuel made from wood raw materials.

Export Activities
